# 戰國配隊室 · S2 港澳台

Independent personal static planner and Traditional Chinese guide. Not affiliated with the game, its publishers, Azea or any business. No game-account connection or backend; your collection stays in this browser's localStorage. Export JSON backups to move data between devices.

Enter your owned generals, tactics, levels and sourced current stats. The Beta candidate comparison keeps the other slots and equipped tactics fixed, checks your entered caps/copy/exclusion rules, and compares role coverage and known stat subtotals. Unknown values remain unknown. It does not calculate official combat power, win rates or battle simulation.

Taiwan's eight historical reference names/mechanisms are from the [2026-01-23 OMG news republication](https://news.omg.com.tw/News/NewsContent?NewsID=11422), classified as media/community evidence. Initial costs and stats are unknown and generals are not marked owned. Hong Kong/Macau catalog starts empty for your sourced entries. [Taiwan official portal](https://nobunaga-shinsen.sialiagamesinc.com.tw/?access=ppgw), [Hong Kong/Macau official portal](https://nobunaga-shinsen.qookkagames.com/).

Current official regional mechanics, uniqueness restrictions, triggering, stacking, troop multipliers and combat formulas are unverified. User-entered uniqueness/exclusion rules are checked. Role coverage does not establish skill compatibility. All graphical namecards are original text designs, not official portraits.

Source and static runtime are the same four files: index.html, app.js, logic.js and style.css. No personal collection, QA files, private reports, credentials or conversations are in this repository. Physical iOS Safari remains untested.
